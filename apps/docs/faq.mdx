<Accordion title="Is Rallly free?">
  Most of Rallly's features are free and many users will never need to pay for
  anything. However, there are some features that are only available to paying
  customers. These features are designed to help you get the most out of Rallly
  and also help pay for the cost of running and maintaining the service.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Do I need to create an account to use Rallly?">
  No, you don't! You can create and share polls without creating an account.
  Without an account you can have one open poll at a time, and some features
  (like email notifications) are only available to users with an account.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Are there any limits to the number of polls, participants and options I can make?">
  There is no limit to the number of polls you can create, the number of
  participants who can vote or the number of options in a poll. What depends on
  your plan is how many polls can be open, collecting votes, at the same time:

  - **Guests** (no account): 1 open poll
  - **Hobby** (free): 3 open polls
  - **Pro**: unlimited open polls

  Finalize or close a poll to make room for another. There are also technical
  limits in place to prevent misuse but these are very generous and should not
  affect normal usage.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What counts as an open poll?">
  A poll is open while it's collecting votes. Polls you have finalized, closed or
  deleted don't count towards your limit, and polls close automatically once all
  their dates have passed. On Hobby the limit applies to the polls you created in
  each space.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How long will Rallly keep my poll?">
  Polls are automatically deleted when all date options are in the past AND there
  has been no activity for over 30 days. Activity includes new votes, comments, or
  changes to the poll. This does not apply to Pro users.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I limit the number of participants that can vote for an option?">
  No, this feature is currently not supported but may be added in the future.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I set a deadline for my poll?">
  No, this feature is currently not supported but may be added in the future.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What if I'm not receiving emails from Rallly?">
  First, check your spam folder.

Not there?

If you're using Outlook, try adding rallly.co to your [safe senders and domains](https://support.microsoft.com/en-gb/office/safe-senders-in-outlook-com-470d4ee6-e3b6-402b-8cd9-a6f00eda7339).

If that doesn't work, it's possible that you've been added to our suppression list.
This can happen if you or your email provider mark an email from Rallly as spam.
If you think this has happened, please [contact us](/contact) and we'll remove you from the list.

</Accordion>
