title: "Invite participants"
description: "Collect responses by sharing the invite link or inviting people by email"
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Share dialog">
    The **Share** dialog opens right after you create your poll. You can get
    back to it at any time by clicking the **Share** button on your poll page.

    To share the **invite link**, click **Copy** and paste it anywhere: chat,
    email or a calendar invite. Anyone with the link can vote on your poll.

    To **invite by email**, enter an email address and click **Send invite**.
    Rallly sends that person an email with a link to your poll.

    <Frame caption="Each invitee shows whether the invite was sent, opened or responded to">
      <img src="/images/workflow/invite-link.png" alt="Share dialog with the invite link and a list of email invitees"/>
    </Frame>

    <Note>Inviting by email requires a [Pro subscription](https://rallly.co/pricing) on Rallly Cloud. Sharing the invite link is free.</Note>

  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for participants to vote">
    Participants open the invite link, pick the options that work for them and
    save their response.

    <Note>Have a look at the [participant guide](/guide/participant-guide) for details on how participants can vote.</Note>

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/workflow/voting.png" alt="Participant voting on a poll"/>
    </Frame>

    As responses come in, you can review the results on your poll page to see
    which dates are preferred. If you invited people by email, the Share
    dialog also shows who has opened their invite and who has responded.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/workflow/review.png" alt="Reviewing poll results"/>
    </Frame>

  </Step>
</Steps>

Once all participants have responded you can proceed with [scheduling your event](/workflow/schedule).
