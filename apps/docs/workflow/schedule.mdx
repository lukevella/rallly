---

<Note>Scheduling requires a [Pro subscription](https://rallly.co/pricing) on Rallly Cloud.</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the schedule dialog">
    Click **Manage** and select **Schedule** from the dropdown menu.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/workflow/finalize.png" alt="Schedule dialog"/>
    </Frame>

  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the final date">
    Each option shows how many participants are available and a breakdown of
    their votes. Select your preferred date and click **Schedule**.

    This will:

    1. Send a calendar invite to you by email.
    2. Send a calendar invite to your participants by email.
    3. Close the poll to new responses.

    The final date is displayed on your poll for everyone to see and can be
    added to a calendar with one click.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/workflow/finalized.png" alt="Scheduled poll"/>
    </Frame>

  </Step>
</Steps>
