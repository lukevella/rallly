title: "Finalize"
description: "Pick the final date, notify your participants and close the poll"
---

Finalizing is available on every plan. It locks in a date, emails a calendar
invite to the participants you choose and closes the poll to new responses.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick the date">
    Click **Finalize** in the poll header. The dates are listed from most to
    least popular, with the top three marked. Each row shows how many
    participants can make it and a breakdown of their votes. Click a date to
    continue.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/workflow/finalize.png" alt="Finalize dialog listing the dates by popularity"/>
    </Frame>

  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose who to notify">
    Everyone who left an email address is selected. Untick anyone you would
    rather tell yourself, or use the checkbox in the header to select or clear
    everyone at once. Participants without an email address are listed but
    cannot be notified.

    This step is skipped when the poll has no participants yet.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/workflow/finalize-notify.png" alt="Notify participants step with the participant list"/>
    </Frame>

  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the summary and finalize">
    The summary shows the date, the location and the video call the event will
    carry. A Zoom, Google Meet or Microsoft Teams link is created at this
    point. Click **Finalize**.

    <Note>
      Zoom and Microsoft Teams links require a [Pro subscription](https://rallly.co/pricing)
      on rallly.co. Google Meet works on every plan.
    </Note>

    This will:

    1. Send a calendar invite to you by email.
    2. Send a calendar invite to the participants you selected. Invites go out
       in batches, so on a large poll the last ones can take a few minutes.
    3. Close the poll to new responses.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/workflow/finalize-summary.png" alt="Summary step before finalizing"/>
    </Frame>

    The final date is displayed on your poll for everyone to see and can be
    added to a calendar with one click.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/workflow/finalized.png" alt="Scheduled poll"/>
    </Frame>

  </Step>
</Steps>
