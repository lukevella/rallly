---
title: "Edit a Poll"
description: "How to make changes to an existing meeting poll."
---

Everything about a poll is edited on one page: its details, its dates and
times, and its settings.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the edit page">
    From the admin page, click **Manage** and select **Edit** from the
    dropdown menu.

    <Frame caption="Admin controls">
      <img src="/images/administrators/edit-poll-menu.png" alt="Manage dropdown menu with the Edit item"/>
    </Frame>

  </Step>
  <Step title="Make your changes">
    The edit page is laid out like the create page and filled in with the
    poll's current values.

    <Frame caption="Edit page">
      <img src="/images/administrators/edit-poll-page.png" alt="Edit poll page"/>
    </Frame>

    **Event** - Change the title, location or description.

    **Calendar** - Add or remove options using the calendar. Switch
    between month and week view, change the duration, or make the poll all
    day.

    **Settings** - Require an email address from participants, hide
    participant names, hide votes until a participant has voted, allow
    comments, and choose the vote options.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save changes**. Only the parts you changed are updated, so a
    poll's responses stay intact when you fix a typo in the title.

    If you remove an option that participants have voted for, Rallly asks you
    to confirm first. Their votes for that option are deleted with it.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Vote options

**Vote options** decides which answers participants can give. By default they
can answer **Yes**, **If need be** or **No**. Switch to **Yes** and **No** if
you want a straight answer with no maybes.

<Note>
  Once a participant has answered **If need be**, you can no longer switch that
  poll to **Yes** and **No**, so their response stays valid. Switching a poll
  back to offering **If need be** is always allowed.
</Note>

## What can't be changed

- **Time zone** - Once votes have been cast, the poll's time zone is locked.
  Changing it would move every option under participants who already answered.
- **Scheduled polls** - A poll that has been scheduled can no longer be edited,
  because the calendar invites your participants received would not pick up
  the change. The **Edit** item is hidden from the **Manage** menu for these
  polls.
