From the admin page, click on **Manage** to open the dropdown menu.

<Frame caption="Admin controls">
  ![](/images/meeting-poll/editing-poll-1.png)
</Frame>

## Changing the title, description or location

To change these details of your poll:

1. Click **Manage**
2. Select **Edit details** from the dropdown menu

<Frame>![](/images/meeting-poll/editing-poll-2.png)</Frame>

3.  Enter the new details of your poll in to the form and click **Save**.

## Changing poll settings

1.  Click **Manage**
2.  Select **Edit settings** from the dropdown menu
3.  Change the settings you want and click **Save**.

**Vote options** decides which answers participants can give. By default they
can answer **Yes**, **If need be** or **No**. Switch to **Yes** and **No** if
you want a straight answer with no maybes.

<Note>
  Once a participant has answered **If need be**, this setting is locked so
  their response stays valid. You can still switch a poll back to offering
  **If need be** at any time.
</Note>

## Adding or removing dates or times

1.  Click **Manage**
2.  Select **Edit options** from the dropdown menu

<Frame caption="Date or time picker">
  ![](/images/meeting-poll/editing-poll-3.png)
</Frame>

3.  Add/remove options using the date/time picker
