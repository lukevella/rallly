---
title: "Delete a poll"
---

<Warning>
  Deleting a poll will delete all data related to that poll such as options,
  participants and votes. This action cannot be undone.
</Warning>

To delete a meeting poll, from your poll page:

1. Click the **⋯** (Manage) button in the poll header
1. Select **Delete** from the dropdown menu

   <Frame>
     <img src="/images/administrators/delete-poll-menu.png" alt="The Manage menu in the poll header with Delete at the bottom" />
   </Frame>

1. Click **Delete** to confirm

   <Frame>
     <img src="/images/administrators/delete-poll-confirm.png" alt="The Delete poll confirmation dialog with Cancel and Delete buttons" />
   </Frame>
