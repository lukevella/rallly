Rallly can now create the meeting link for you. Connect Google Meet, Zoom, Microsoft Teams or Webex, add a video call to your poll, and when you finalize, Rallly creates the meeting for the chosen time and puts the join link in the event, the calendar invite and the confirmation emails.

No more creating a meeting by hand once the votes are in and pasting the link into a follow up email.

![The Add video call menu on the poll form, listing the connected and available providers](https://support.rallly.co/images/integrations/zoom/video-call-menu.png)

## How it works

1. Open **Settings** and choose **Conferencing**.
2. Click **Connect** next to the provider you use and approve the connection.
3. When you create a poll, click **Add video call** and choose that provider. Participants see which service you'll be using while they vote.
4. When you finalize, the meeting is created on your account for the chosen time.

Everyone you notify gets the video call link in their confirmation email and calendar invite, so there's nothing to send separately. Participants can also join straight from the poll page.

![A finalized poll showing a Zoom call with a Join link above the scheduled date](/static/images/video-call-integrations/event-card.png)

The meeting is created on the account you connected, so your usual settings, like waiting rooms, passcodes and lobby rules, still apply.

Google Meet works on every plan, including free. Zoom, Microsoft Teams and Webex are part of Pro.

If you'd rather use something else, you can still add any video call with your own link.
