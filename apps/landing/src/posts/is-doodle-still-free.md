Short answer: yes, Doodle still has a free plan, but it now allows just **one group poll with up to 10 time slots**. You and the people you invite see ads, and reminders and deadlines are paid features. If you're asking this question, you've probably noticed that the free plan doesn't do what it used to. You're not imagining it.

- **One group poll**
- One booking page

## What changed

Doodle built its reputation as the free way to find a time that works for a group. Over the past few years, the focus has shifted. The free plan shrank step by step, and the changes came gradually rather than in a single announcement:

- **Polls:** the free plan went from unlimited group polls to one.
- **Time slots:** free polls were capped at 20 options and are now capped at 10.
- **Ads:** ads became more prominent and are shown to participants as well as organizers.
- **Settings:** deadlines, reminders and hiding participant details moved to paid plans.
- **Mobile app:** in June 2025, Doodle [paused its iOS and Android apps](https://doodle.com/en/doodle-mobile-app-change/) while it rebuilds them. You can still use Doodle in a mobile browser.

The product is now aimed at teams and organizations, with plans running from Pro up to an Enterprise tier that starts at $15,000 a year.

### Doodle's own pages don't agree

If you've found conflicting answers to this question, part of the reason is that Doodle publishes conflicting answers itself. Here's what each of its pages said when we checked in October 2026:

- The [pricing page](https://doodle.com/en/premium/) says the free plan includes "One Group Poll".
- An article on Doodle's blog, [Free scheduling tools 2026](https://doodle.com/en/free-scheduling-tools-2026), describes the free plan as including "Unlimited group polls".
- The help centre's [free vs. Professional comparison](https://help.doodle.com/en/articles/10448913-doodle-free-and-doodle-professional-differences-at-a-glance), dated July 2026, says free polls are limited to 10 time slots.
- An older [group poll FAQ](https://help.doodle.com/en/articles/10295132-group-poll-troubleshooting-and-faq) in the same help centre still says 20.

The pricing page is where Doodle sells its plans, and the help centre comparison is the only page that lists every limit side by side, so those are the numbers we've used above. The blog article is a marketing roundup, not a plan description.

## Is Doodle's free plan enough for you?

It depends on how often you schedule. The free plan works if one poll is all you need, with no more than 10 options to choose from, and you don't mind your participants seeing ads.

It stops working if you need more than one poll, like a committee meeting and a social event in the same month. It also stops working if you want to offer more than 10 dates, if you need to send reminders to people who haven't voted, or if you're sharing the poll with clients and would rather they didn't see ads.

That's a legitimate business choice, and if your company schedules client meetings all day, Doodle's paid plans may serve you well. But if you're the person who occasionally needs to find a time for a book club, a committee, a rehearsal, or a team catch up, you're no longer who Doodle is building for.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Doodle free?

Yes. Doodle has a free plan that doesn't need a card. It's limited to one group poll with up to 10 time slots, one booking page and one 1:1, and it shows ads to you and your participants.

### How many polls can you make on Doodle for free?

One group poll. Unlimited group polls start on the Pro plan.

### How many time slots can a free Doodle poll have?

Ten, according to Doodle's help centre. Doodle Pro allows up to 1,000.

### Does Doodle's free plan have ads?

Yes. On the free plan, ads are shown to the organizer and to participants who don't have a paid Doodle account. Removing them requires a paid plan.

### How much does Doodle Pro cost?

$11 per seat per month if you pay for a year upfront, which is $132 a year. Paying monthly works out to about $15 per month.

### Does Doodle still have a mobile app?

Not at the moment. Doodle paused its iOS and Android apps in June 2025 while it rebuilds them. The website works in a mobile browser.

### Is there a free alternative to Doodle?

Yes. [Rallly](https://rallly.co) is a free, open source scheduling poll with no ads. You can create as many polls as you like, with up to 3 open at a time, and participants vote without an account.
