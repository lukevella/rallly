Short answer: yes, Doodle still has a free plan, but it now allows just **one group poll**. If you're asking this question, you've probably noticed that the free plan doesn't do what it used to. You're not imagining it.

## What Doodle's free plan includes in 2026

As of September 2026, Doodle's [pricing page](https://doodle.com/en/premium/) lists these limits on the free plan:

- **One group poll**
- One booking page
- One 1:1
- Basic sign-up sheets

![Doodle's pricing page in September 2026, showing the free plan limited to one group poll](/static/images/is-doodle-still-free/doodle-pricing-september-2026.png)

Everything else requires a paid plan, including features that used to be part of the core experience:

- **No ads** is a paid feature. On the free plan, both you and the people you invite see ads.
- **Deadlines and reminders** are paid.
- **Hiding participant details** is paid.
- Unlimited polls start at **Pro**, which costs $15 per month, or $11 per month if you pay for a year upfront.

## What changed

Doodle built its reputation as the free way to find a time that works for a group. Over the past few years, the focus has shifted. The free plan shrank step by step, ads became more prominent, and the product is now aimed at teams and organizations. Their plans today run from Pro at $11 per seat per month up to an Enterprise tier that starts at $15,000 per year.

That's a legitimate business choice, and if your company schedules client meetings all day, Doodle's paid plans may serve you well. But if you're the person who occasionally needs to find a time for a book club, a committee, a rehearsal, or a team catch-up, you're no longer who Doodle is building for.
