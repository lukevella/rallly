---
title: Introducing email invites
date: "2026-09-04"
category: Product
excerpt: Invite people to your poll by email and see who has opened it and who has responded.
---

Until now there was one way to get a poll in front of people: copy the link and paste it somewhere. That works, but it leaves you guessing. You don't know who has seen it, and chasing the people who haven't responded means keeping your own list on the side.

You can now invite people by email from the poll page. Open the Share dialog, enter an address and press Send invite. Each person gets an email with their own link to the poll.

![The Share dialog with an invite link, an email field and three invited people marked Sent, Opened and Responded](/static/images/email-invites/invite-statuses.png)

Everyone you invite shows up in the dialog with their current status. Sent means the email went out. Opened means they clicked the link. Responded means they submitted their availability. You can see at a glance who still needs a nudge.

The invite link is still there and still free. Email invites are part of Rallly Pro, so if you already have a Pro subscription or belong to a Pro space, it's on your polls now. You'll need to be signed in to send invites, since they and the responses they bring in are tied to your account rather than to the browser you happen to be using.

This is the first step towards making Rallly better at the part of scheduling that happens after you create the poll. Reminders for people who haven't responded are next.
