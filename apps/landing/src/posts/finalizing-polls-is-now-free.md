Rallly's free plan now covers the whole job of scheduling something, from creating the poll to sending everyone the final date. In exchange, the free plan has a limit on how many polls can be open at the same time.

## Finalize on every plan

Finalizing used to be a Pro feature. It isn't anymore. Click **Finalize** in the poll header, pick a date from the list of options ranked by popularity, choose who to notify and confirm.

![The summary step of the Finalize dialog, showing the chosen date, location and video call before finalizing](/static/images/free-scheduling/finalize-summary.png)

Everyone you select gets an email with a calendar invite, so the event lands in their calendar without anyone copying details by hand. If the poll has a video call, the link is created when you finalize and included in the invite. Google Meet works on every plan. Zoom, Microsoft Teams and Webex are part of Pro.

Finalizing is the step that gets the meeting into people's calendars. With it behind a paywall, the free plan stopped one step short of actually scheduling anything.

## Open poll limits

What now differs between plans is how many polls you can have open, collecting votes, at the same time. Without an account you can have one open poll. A free account can have three. Pro has no limit.

There is still no limit on how many polls you can create, how many people can vote or how many options a poll can have. A poll stops counting as soon as you finalize or close it, and polls close on their own once all their dates have passed. If you organize the occasional dinner or team meeting, you're unlikely to notice the limit at all.

If you're scheduling with lots of groups at once, which tends to be the case when scheduling is part of your job, Pro removes the limit along with the other Pro features like email invites, custom branding and Zoom and Teams links.

## Why the change

I want Rallly to stay free for people who schedule casually, and I also need it to pay for itself so I can keep building it. Charging for one step of the workflow did neither very well. Charging for volume is a closer match to how much value people actually get out of Rallly, and it means the free plan works end to end.

I'm still experimenting with how the plans are structured and I'll keep adjusting based on how this goes. If you have thoughts, or the limit gets in your way, I'd like to hear about it at [feedback@rallly.co](mailto:feedback@rallly.co).

You can read more about [finalizing a poll](https://support.rallly.co/workflow/schedule) in the docs, or compare plans on the [pricing page](/pricing).
